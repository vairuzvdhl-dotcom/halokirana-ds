## Product Purpose

Kirana (operating as HaloKirana) is an AI‑driven omnichannel chat platform that unifies TikTok comments, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and other messaging apps into a single inbox. Their virtual assistant, Kirana, automatically reads product catalogs and stock levels to reply to customer inquiries in the brand’s own tone, handles order details, pricing, and checkout, and follows up on abandoned carts. The service offers real‑time notifications, auto‑reply templates, and seamless hand‑off to human agents when needed. Targeting F&B, retail, home‑care, aviation, and public‑service businesses across Indonesia, Kirana helps companies reduce response time, increase conversion rates, and eliminate the need for manual, repetitive messaging.
